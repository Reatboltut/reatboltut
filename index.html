Overview

A web-based platform that helps a church organize its digital presence and manage important information in one place: members, services, events, attendance, announcements, leadership and organizational content. It was designed for simplicity, accessibility and practical church administration.

The problem

Church information and records become hard to manage when they are spread across paper documents, spreadsheets and different communication channels.

The solution

A centralized, responsive web platform that presents church information and organizes church management functions in clear sections that non-technical users can understand.

My role

  • Frontend: designed the interface, built responsive layouts, navigation and interactive elements.
  • Database design: planned the structure for church records and the relationships between members, activities and other data, with future growth in mind.
  • UI/UX: a clean, church-oriented visual style with information organized into clear sections.
